Crypto Lab

AES-GCM and RSA-OAEP encryption run entirely in this browser. Do not share secrets.

AES-GCM (256-bit only)

Uses a Base64 256-bit key and a required Base64 12-byte IV. Each encryption creates a fresh IV and keeps the used IV here for decryption. AES-GCM authenticates ciphertext.

RSA-OAEP (SHA-256 only)

Encryption accepts an SPKI public-key PEM; decryption accepts a PKCS#8 private-key PEM.
