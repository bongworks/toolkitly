Privacy Policy

How Bongworks handles information when you use Toolkitly.

Operator

Toolkitly is operated by Bongworks, an individual operator.

Tool input

JSON and other values entered into currently available tools are processed only in your browser. Toolkitly does not upload, store, or sell that input.

Analytics

Operator requirement: before enabling GA4, disable Enhanced Measurement and automatic event collection in the GA4 data stream and Google tag configuration, including form interactions, site search, outbound clicks, and file downloads. This site's code cannot enforce account-side settings. The limited collection described below depends on this configuration.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) loads when configured by the operator. In regions or configurations requiring consent, it waits for your explicit consent. Our analytics events contain only a known page path, tool identifier, and a general action category (run, copy, or download). They do not include tool inputs, results, query strings, URL fragments, referrers, clipboard contents, or error messages. When enabled, Google may also process device and browser information, approximate location, cookies, and online identifiers according to the service settings.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense Auto ads load when configured by the operator. In regions or configurations requiring consent, they wait for your explicit consent. When enabled, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or online identifiers to serve ads, including personalized ads where permitted by your choices and the service settings.

Google and international processing

GA4 and AdSense are provided by Google. When these integrations are enabled, information handled by them may be processed by Google and its affiliates outside your country.

Read how Google uses information from partner sites.

Your choices

In regions or configurations requiring consent, use the operator's consent control to choose whether to enable analytics and advertising. These integrations remain disabled until you explicitly consent in that mode. You can also manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings and change cookie settings in your browser.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact service.bongworks@gmail.com.

Effective date: September 18, 2026. We will update this policy before changing our data practices.
